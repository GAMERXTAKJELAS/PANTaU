Devices

Supported devices & metrics

Pulse Oximeter

Track oxygen saturation (SpO2 %) and pulse rate (BPM) quickly.

Blood Pressure Monitor

Record Systolic / Diastolic (mmHg) and Pulse (BPM) with a built-in 1-minute rest timer for an optional second reading.

Blood Glucose Monitor

Log blood sugar levels defaulting to Malaysia's standard mmol/L (with mg/dL option).

How it works

Three simple steps

1

Quick MyKad Setup

Sign up with your Name, IC Number, and PIN — age and gender are calculated automatically.

2

Flexible Logging & Drafts

Add readings one device at a time via the + button, save drafts temporarily as you measure, and tag daily notes (Before Eating, Relaxing, etc.).

3

Timestamped History & PDF Export

View automatically dated and timed records and print or download clean PDF reports for clinic visits.
